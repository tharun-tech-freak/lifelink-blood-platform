# Problem Statement

During blood emergencies, patients and their families often struggle to find suitable voluntary donors quickly. Information is usually scattered across phone calls, messaging groups, and social networks.

Existing platforms may provide blood availability information, but they do not always provide a complete community-based workflow for donor discovery, request verification, donor responses, remote donor coordination, and long-term donor engagement.

LifeLink is a full-stack blood donor coordination and community support platform. It allows one user to act as both a donor and a requester. Users can create verified blood requests for themselves, family members, or friends. Available donors can find compatible requests based on blood group and location and respond through the platform.

When a compatible donor is far from the recipient hospital, LifeLink can suggest a nearby verified partner blood bank or donation centre for the donor. The donor can donate at the assigned authorized centre instead of travelling to the recipient hospital. The partner blood bank is responsible for donor screening, blood collection, testing, component processing, storage, cold-chain handling, and any permitted coordination with the recipient hospital or blood bank.

The platform also includes a non-monetary LifeToken system. Verified donors receive tokens, badges, and community recognition for their contribution. These tokens may support faster platform-level coordination in future emergencies, but they do not guarantee blood availability or replace the procedures of authorized blood banks.

LifeLink is intended to support coordination only. Final donor eligibility, medical screening, blood collection, testing, storage, compatibility checks, component processing, transport, and issue of blood must be handled by authorized healthcare professionals and blood banks.
